Detailed Change Log:
Systems:
Added Trainer Battles.
Added Dynamaxing with the Dynamax Band item. Functions similarly to the mega cuff. Still in early stages of development. 
New Move Tutor NPC
Arrives in town after a pokemon forgets a move.
Can teach a pokemon moves it has forgotten, for a small fee.
Added a config option for stat scaling to help make Pokemod compatible with other mods such as Calamity.
Small Pokemon can now be picked up.
Some big Pokemon can now be ridden.
Basic implementation of status conditions
Some Pokemon now have loot drops
Some Pokemon now use Vanilla Worm like behavior.
Gender Implementation

Items:
Added the Dynamax Band (8 Hallowed Bars)
Improvements to the trainer glove which now buffs pokemon attacks when used.
New Trainer glove varieties:
Blue Trainer Glove (7 silk, 7 flinx fur, 5 water candles)
Green Trainer Glove (7 silk, 7 jungle spores, 5 bee wax)
Red Trainer Glove (7 silk, 7 hellstone, 5 obsidian)
Great Trainer Glove (7 silk, 5 demonite/crimtane bars)
Hyper Trainer Glove (7 silk, 5 bones, 5 gold/platinum bars)
New Accessories:
Added Type Gems, accessories that boost attacks of their given type (drops from Pokemon)
Tera Gem boosts all pokemon damage (crafted from one of each other gem).
Added several similar accessories, each boosting damage of a certain type:
Black Belt was edited (3 Fighting Gems and a Water Candle)
Black Glasses, (3 Dark Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Charcoal, (3 Fire Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Dragon Fang, (3 Dragon Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Fairy Feather, (3 Fairy Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Hard Stone, (3 Rock Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Magnet, (3 Electric Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Metal Coat, (3 Steel Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Miracle Seed, (3 Grass Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Mystic Water, (3 Water Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Never Melt Ice, (3 Ice Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Poison Barb, (3 Poison Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Sharp Beak, (3 Flying Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Silk Scarf, (3 Normal Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Silver Powder, (3 Bug Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Soft Sand, (3 Ground Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Spell Tag, (3 Ghost Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Twisted Spoon, (3 Psychic Gems, 1 Water Candle)
Big Root, (3 Vines, 10 Mushroom Roots, 3 Bottled Honey)
Muscle Band (5 Hellstone Bars, 10 Soul of Night, 10 Soul of Light)
Shell Bell (15 Seashells, 5 Chains)
TMs are refunded if the move is not learned.
Mega stone changes:
Added Mega Shards (Found in meteor craters and mined with a cobalt pick or better)
Mega stones are no longer sold by Prof Oak, but are now crafted using mega shards, type gems, and various hardmode souls (Night, Flight, Might, etc).
The key stone crafting recipe also now uses mega shards instead of a white pearl. 
Added skull fossil for reviving Cranidos (found among primordial fossil blocks)
